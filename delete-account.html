Delete your Kuyeza account

You can delete your account and personal data yourself, or ask us to do it.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Kuyeza and tap Profile.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Read the message and tap Delete account again to confirm.

Option 2: ask us by email

If you no longer have the app, email admin@rehumile.co.za from your own email address with the subject Delete my Kuyeza account and the cellphone number you signed in with. We will confirm that the number is yours, delete the account and reply to tell you it is done. We aim to do this within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and cellphone number.
  • Your saved delivery addresses.
  • Your sign-in. You will need to sign up again to use Kuyeza.
  • Your cart, saved orders and sync data on your phone.

What we keep

  • Past orders are kept, but without your name, cellphone number or delivery address, so that shops, refunds and tax records stay correct. They can no longer be linked to you.

Deletion cannot be undone. See our privacy policy for how we handle your information.
