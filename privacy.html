Privacy policy

Draft of 18 November 2026. This policy applies to the Kuyeza app. It is written for the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

Who is responsible

The responsible party is Kuyeza. Contact us at admin@rehumile.co.za.

What we collect and why

  • Cellphone number. To sign you in with a one-time code and to link orders to you.
  • Delivery addresses and, if you allow it, your location. To find and save where your order should be delivered. Location is used only while you use the app and only when you give permission.
  • Orders. What you ordered, the total, the delivery time and place, and the order status.
  • Cart and settings on your phone. Kept in the app on your device so it works offline.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase, which hosts our database and sign-in.
  • Clickatell, which sends the one-time code to your cellphone as an SMS.
  • The shop you order from, who needs your order and delivery details to fulfil it.

When online payments are added we will name the payment provider here before they are used. Card details are handled by the payment provider and are never stored by us.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information until you delete your account. Past orders are kept without your name, cellphone number or delivery address after you delete your account, so that shops, refunds and tax records stay correct.

Your rights

You may ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. You can delete your account in the app (how to) or by emailing us. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so that one customer cannot see another customer's orders or addresses.

Children

Kuyeza is not intended for children under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date at the top.

Kuyeza, South Africa. admin@rehumile.co.za
